How likely is Cyclospora in your food?



There is a wave of cyclosporiasis currently happening in the United States. Cyclosporiasis is caused by a parasite called Cyclospora. Symptoms of cyclosporiasis include watery diarrhea that relapses multiple times, nausea, fatigue, and stomach cramping among others. Cyclosporiasis is typically contracted when someone eats cyclospora oocysts in their food. This app will help you determine what the risk of contracting cyclospora from your food and how to minimize the likelihood of illness. Peeled Fruit

This food is LOW RISK for cyclospora.

Removing the peel from fruits like apples, oranges, and bananas is considered an effective way to lower the risk of Cyclospora.

Thoroughly washing and removing the peel from fruit should considerably lower any risk of Cyclospora. If concern remains, cooking to an internal temperature of 158F/70C should remove any added risk.

Pre-washed Vegetables

This food is a HIGH RISK for cyclospora.

Pre-washed vetables like packaged salad greens and salad kits are considered very high risk for Cyclospora, even if rinsed again and have been previously linked to outbreaks.

Safer options may be to buy whole greens which are then washed thoroughly and have their outer leaves removed before consuming. Additionally cooking greens to an internal temperature of 158F/70C should lower risk.
